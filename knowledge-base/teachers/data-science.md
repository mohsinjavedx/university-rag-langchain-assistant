# Department of Data Science (DDS) Faculty — PUCIT / FCIT New Campus

Established as an independent department within the Faculty of Computing & Information Technology (FCIT) in January 2021, the Department of Data Science operates at the Quaid-i-Azam (New) Campus, Lahore. The department conducts advanced teaching and research across data engineering, predictive modeling, NLP, deep learning, and process analytics.

## Core Academic Offerings & Research Labs
* **Degree Programs Hosted**: BS Data Science (BSDS), MS Data Science (MSDS), and interdisciplinary PhD research.
* **Specialized Research Groups**:
  * **Natural Language Processing Research Group (NLPRG)**: Specializing in low-resource language processing (Urdu and regional languages).
  * **Business Process & Data Engineering Lab**: Specializing in enterprise log analytics and predictive maintenance.
  * **Techtics AI Lab & Collaborative Center**: Bridging student research projects with real-world enterprise applications in health-tech and fintech.
