# Department of Computer Science (DCS) Faculty — PUCIT / FCIT New Campus

The Department of Computer Science at the Punjab University College of Information Technology (PUCIT), now under the Faculty of Computing & Information Technology (FCIT), University of the Punjab, is located at the Quaid-i-Azam (New) Campus on Canal Road / Samsani Road, Lahore.

The department is dedicated to high-impact research, undergraduate (BSCS), and postgraduate education (MS/MPhil and PhD in Computer Science).

---

## Academic Focus & Core Courses Taught
* **CS Core**: Programming Fundamentals, Object Oriented Programming, Data Structures & Algorithms, Design and Analysis of Algorithms.
* **Systems Domain**: Operating Systems, Computer Architecture & Organization, Computer Networks, Distributed Computing.
* **Theoretical Domain**: Discrete Mathematical Structures, Theory of Automata, Compiler Construction, Analysis of Algorithms.
* **Intelligent Systems**: Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Computer Vision, Digital Image Processing, Natural Language Processing.
