# Department of Software Engineering (DSE) Faculty — PUCIT / FCIT New Campus

The Department of Software Engineering (DSE) at PUCIT / FCIT (Quaid-i-Azam Campus, Lahore) prepares engineers and researchers in modern software construction, enterprise system architecture, software testing, Agile practices, and human-computer interaction. It houses the centralized Final Year Design Project (FYDP) Office.

## Areas of Research & Practical Training
1. **Software Architecture & Enterprise Design**: Design patterns, Microservices, Component-based architectures, and DevOps pipelines.
2. **Software Verification & Quality Assurance (QA)**: Automated unit testing, test-driven development (TDD), regression test optimization, and performance benchmarking.
3. **Human-Computer Interaction (HCI)**: User research, usability heuristic evaluation, accessibility compliance, and UI/UX design.
4. **Agile Methodologies & Project Management**: Scrum workflows, backlog grooming, requirement traceability, and version control governance.
