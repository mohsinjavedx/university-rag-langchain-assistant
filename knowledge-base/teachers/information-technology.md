# Department of Information Technology (DIT) Faculty — PUCIT / FCIT New Campus

The Department of Information Technology (DIT) at PUCIT / FCIT, Quaid-i-Azam Campus, Lahore, is a primary center for education in network engineering, cybersecurity, cloud architecture, system administration, and practical applications of Artificial Intelligence and multi-agent systems.

## Technical Disciplines & Laboratory Focus
1. **Networking & Security**: Routing & Switching (Cisco/Juniper configurations), IPv6 transition protocols, VPN setups, firewall auditing.
2. **System Administration & Cloud Ops**: Linux server environments (Ubuntu/RHEL), Docker containerization, Kubernetes orchestration, virtualized environments.
3. **Applied AI & Agent-Based Modeling**: Crowd panic simulations, multi-agent behavioral analysis, intelligent routing agents.
4. **Information Architecture**: Web technologies, REST APIs, enterprise storage systems, disaster recovery planning.
