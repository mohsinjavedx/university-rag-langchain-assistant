# Admissions, Merit Criteria, Fee Structure and Scholarships — PUCIT / FCIT New Campus

Admission to PUCIT / FCIT at Quaid-i-Azam Campus is among the most competitive in Pakistan, attracting tens of thousands of applicants annually for limited seats in BS Computer Science, Software Engineering, IT, and Data Science.

---

## Eligibility Criteria for Undergraduate Programs (BS)

Applicants must meet one of the following academic qualifications:
1. **FSc Pre-Engineering**: Physics, Mathematics, Chemistry (minimum 50% or 60% aggregate depending on yearly prospectus guidelines).
2. **ICS (Intermediate in Computer Science)**: Physics/Statistics, Mathematics, Computer Science.
3. **General Science / Pre-Medical with Additional Math**: FSc Pre-Medical students who have cleared Mathematics at the intermediate level.
4. **A-Level / High School Diploma**: Cambridge A-Levels or US High School Diploma with Equivalence Certificate from the Inter Board Coordination Commission (IBCC), Islamabad, including Mathematics.

---

## Merit Calculation Formula
The final merit aggregate for admission is calculated strictly on quantitative weightage determined by the University of the Punjab Academic Council:

$$\text{Final Merit Aggregate} = 25\% \times (\text{Matric / O-Level \%}) + 45\% \times (\text{Intermediate Part-I / FSc \%}) + 30\% \times (\text{PU Admission Test \%})$$

* *Hafiz-e-Quran*: Eligible candidates can receive up to 20 additional marks upon passing the university's oral Quran recitation test.
* Deduction: 2 marks are deducted per year of academic gap from the total percentage for candidates who cleared intermediate in prior examination sessions.

## Scholarships & Financial Assistance Programs

PUCIT / FCIT provides robust safety nets to ensure financial distress never cuts short a student's degree:
1. **HEC Need-Based Scholarships**: Covers 100% of university tuition fees plus a monthly stipend for boarding and textbooks throughout 4 academic years.
2. **Punjab Educational Endowment Fund (PEEF)**: Awarded to deserving students residing in Punjab who have secured top intermediate marks.
3. **PUCIT Alumni Scholarship Fund**: Managed through generous endowments by overseas and Pakistani alumni, offering emergency fee vouchers to students facing sudden family hardships.
4. **Fee Concession & Kinship Waivers**: Partial to full tuition fee waivers awarded by the FCIT Financial Aid Committee based on household income verification.
5. **Ehsaas / National Undergraduate Scholarship Program**: Federal financial support initiatives for lower-income students.

## Application Workflow
1. **Online Portal Registration**: Applications are submitted via the Punjab University Online Admission Portal (`pu.edu.pk/admissions`).
2. **Bank Voucher Payment**: Challan forms are payable at any designated Habib Bank Limited (HBL) or United Bank Limited (UBL) branch across Pakistan.
3. **Merit Lists Display**: 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th, and 5th computerized merit lists are published on the official PUCIT portal ([PUCIT Merit Lists Archive](https://pucit.edu.pk/)).
4. **Physical Verification**: Selected candidates submit original documents, medical fitness certificates, and fee receipts at the New Campus Admissions Desk.
