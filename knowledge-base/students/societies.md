# Student Societies, Co-Curricular Clubs and Flagship Events — PUCIT / FCIT New Campus

Student life at PUCIT New Campus is marked by a vibrant culture of co-curricular societies, hackathons, sports tournaments, literary contests, and community outreach. Societies are student-led under the mentorship of appointed faculty advisors.

---

## Technical & Professional Societies

### 1. PUCIT-ACM Student Chapter & ACM-W
* **Affiliation**: Association for Computing Machinery (ACM), USA.
* **Faculty Advisor**: Prof. Dr. Syed Waqar ul Qounain Jaffry.
* **Scope**: Flagship technical chapter known for organizing programming contests, mobile app and web development bootcamps, and technical seminars.
* **International Recognition**: Won ACM Student Chapter Excellence Awards for Outstanding Chapter Website and Chapter Activities ([ACM Official Awards Archive](https://www.acm.org/binaries/content/assets/chapters/student-chapter-excellence-awards-submissions-2016/outstanding-chapter-website-submissions/pucit-acm-student-chapter-website.pdf)).
* **ACM-W Wing**: Dedicated to empowering female students in computing, hosting women-in-tech panels, mentoring circles, and coding contests.

### 2. IEEE Student Branch PUCIT
* **Affiliation**: Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE).
* **Focus**: Embedded systems, IoT, electronics workshops, research paper writing sessions, and professional networking with industry engineering leaders.

### 3. Debuggers Society / Coding Club
* **Focus**: Competitive programming mastery, algorithms problem-solving, and mentoring student teams for national and international contests (e.g., ICPC Regional, Google Hash Code, IEEEXtreme, Codeforces, LeetCode sprints).

---

## Cultural, Sports & Welfare Societies

### 4. PUCIT Art & Literary Society (PALS)
* **Scope**: Serves as the creative and cultural soul of the student body.
* **Wings**:
  * **Debates Wing**: English and Urdu parliamentary debate teams representing Punjab University across national circuits.
  * **Dramatics & Theatrics Wing**: Annual plays, street theatre, and short films.
  * **Literary & Poetry Wing (Bait Bazi / Mushaira)**: Annual poetry galas, essay writing competitions, and book reading clubs.
  * **Visual Arts & Photography Wing**: Sketching, painting, digital illustrations, and campus photography exhibitions.

### 5. PUCIT Sports Society
* **Faculty Advisor**: Dr. Syed Faisal Bukhari.
* **Activities**:
  * Organizes the massive annual **PUCIT Sports Gala** at the New Campus sports grounds.
  * Competitions: Cricket (Tape-ball & Hard-ball), Futsal / Football, Table Tennis, Badminton, Basketball, Volleyball, Chess, and Athletics.
  * Inter-university representation at Punjab University inter-collegiate championships.

### 6. PUCIT Blood Donating Society (BDS)
* **Mission**: Humanitarian welfare organization maintaining an emergency 24/7 blood donor database.
* **Operations**: Arranges bi-annual blood donation camps in collaboration with Shaukat Khanum Memorial Hospital, Sundas Foundation, and Fatimid Foundation, delivering hundreds of blood bags for thalassemia patients.

### 7. Event Management Society (EMS)
* **Scope**: Manages campus event logistics, protocol, stage coordination, and VIP hospitality for orientations, convocation ceremonies, career fairs, and musical evenings.

## Flagship Annual Events

### SoftExpo
* Pakistan's oldest and most renowned student-organized computing expo.
* Features software project competitions, algorithmic coding showdowns, speed programming, ROBO wars, gaming tournaments (FIFA, CS:GO, Tekken), and graphic design showcases.
* Attracts participants from over 50+ Pakistani universities and institutes.

### PUCON (PUCIT Computing Conference & Career Fair)
* Multi-track technology conference featuring keynote speeches from Silicon Valley engineers, Pakistani tech CEOs, and academic researchers.
* Hosts the **FCIT Annual Career Fair**, bringing 70+ top software houses (Systems Ltd, NetSol, Arbisoft, Confiz, 10Pearls, VentureDive, Tkxel) to conduct on-campus interviews and hire graduating seniors.
