# Campus Facilities, Infrastructure and Student Life — PUCIT / FCIT New Campus

The Quaid-i-Azam Campus (famously referred to as "New Campus") of PUCIT / FCIT spans a serene, expansive environment along Canal Bank Road / Samsani Road in southern Lahore. Covering hundreds of green acres with fruit orchards, quiet pathways, modern academic blocks, and dedicated sports infrastructure, it provides a peaceful learning atmosphere distinct from the bustling urban setting of the Old Campus (Allama Iqbal Campus, Anarkali).

---

## Physical Infrastructure & Facilities

### Academic & Lab Complex
* **Modern Lecture Theatres**: Tiered, multimedia-fitted lecture theatres equipped with high-lumen projectors, smart audio systems, acoustic wall paneling, and air conditioning.
* **Specialized Computing Laboratories**: 12+ purpose-built computer labs with gigabit LAN connections, dual-boot Linux/Windows workstations, and dedicated UPS power back-ups.
* **GIS Centre**: Specialized laboratory infrastructure for Geographic Information Systems and remote sensing data analysis ([Punjab University Press](https://pu.edu.pk/home/section/press/1115)).
* **Software Technology Park (STP) & Incubation Center**: Established through PUCIT development funds to house tech company branch offices, foster student startup incubation, and deepen industry-academia linkages ([PUCIT STP Ground Breaking](https://pu.edu.pk/home/section/press/8766)).

### Student Amenities & Dining
* **Cafeteria & Food Stalls**: On-campus canteen offering affordable hot meals, chai/coffee, snacks, fresh fruit juices, and student socializing areas.
* **Prayer Halls / Mosque**: Spacious, carpeted prayer areas with dedicated ablution facilities for male and female students and staff.
* **Day Care Center**: Dedicated facility on-site supporting faculty, staff, and student mothers.
* **Student Facilitation Desks**: One-window facilitation for fee vouchers, document attestations, and ID card queries.

---

## Sports Infrastructure
The vast grounds at New Campus provide students with outdoor sports facilities unmatched by most urban computer science campuses:
* **Cricket Grounds**: Full-sized cricket pitch used for inter-departmental tournaments and hard-ball / tape-ball championships.
* **Football & Futsal Grounds**: Grass turf football fields host intra-university league matches.
* **Indoor Sports Rooms**: Multiple table tennis tables, carrom boards, and chess corners located in student common rooms.
* **Basketball & Volleyball Courts**: Dedicated hard courts with night lighting for evening practice.

## Transportation & Hostel Accommodations

### University Bus Fleet ("Points")
* The University of the Punjab operates one of the largest subsidized student bus fleets in Pakistan, commonly known as **"PU Points"**.
* Buses service all key residential zones and suburbs across Lahore, including Johar Town, Wapda Town, Thokar Niaz Baig, Model Town, Gulberg, Iqbal Town, Cantt, Shahdara, and Old Campus (Bahawalpur Block / Anarkali).
* Scheduled departures operate early morning (for morning shift classes), afternoon (for midday commuters), and evening (for self-supporting students).

### Residential Hostels (Quaid-i-Azam Campus)
* Out-of-city students admitted on merit are eligible for hostel allotment within the designated Boys Hostels and Girls Hostels situated across Quaid-i-Azam Campus.
* Hostels feature furnished rooms, mess halls serving 3 daily meals, Wi-Fi connectivity, laundry facilities, study rooms, and round-the-clock university security surveillance.
