# Academic Degree Programs and Curriculum — PUCIT / FCIT New Campus

PUCIT / FCIT at Quaid-i-Azam Campus offers rigorous, industry-aligned computing education approved by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan and accredited in the highest tier (**Category-X**) by the National Computing Education Accreditation Council (NCEAC).

---

## Undergraduate Degree Programs (4-Year / 8 Semesters)

All BS programs require completion of **130 to 136 credit hours**, distributed across general education courses, university computing core, domain core, domain electives, and a mandatory two-semester Final Year Design Project (FYDP).

### 1. BS Computer Science (BSCS)
* **Accreditation**: NCEAC Category-X.
* **Objective**: Provides an in-depth theoretical and algorithmic foundation combined with practical software construction skills.
* **Core Curriculum**:
  * Computing Core: Programming Fundamentals, Object Oriented Programming, Data Structures & Algorithms, Discrete Structures, Operating Systems, Database Systems, Software Engineering, Computer Networks, Information Security.
  * CS Domain Core: Design and Analysis of Algorithms, Theory of Automata, Compiler Construction, Computer Architecture & Assembly Language, Artificial Intelligence, Parallel and Distributed Computing.
  * Electives: Computer Vision, Deep Learning, Natural Language Processing, Cyber Security, Distributed Systems.
* **Shifts Offered**: Morning and Self-Supporting (Afternoon).

### 2. BS Software Engineering (BSSE)
* **Accreditation**: NCEAC Category-X.
* **Objective**: Focuses on large-scale software system design, engineering principles, quality assurance, and project life cycles.
* **Core Curriculum**:
  * SE Domain Core: Software Requirements Engineering (SRE), Software Design & Architecture (SDA), Software Quality Engineering (SQE), Software Project Management (SPM), Web Engineering, Human-Computer Interaction (HCI).
  * Electives: Mobile Application Development, Enterprise Systems Development, Cloud Computing, Agile Software Engineering, Formal Methods.

### 3. BS Information Technology (BSIT)
* **Accreditation**: NCEAC Category-X.
* **Objective**: Prepares IT specialists capable of deploying, maintaining, and securing modern IT infrastructure, networked services, and enterprise databases.
* **Core Curriculum**:
  * IT Domain Core: System & Network Administration, Network Security, Enterprise Systems, Database Administration & Management, Web Technologies, Virtualization & Cloud Services.
  * Electives: Cyber Forensics, Cloud Infrastructure Management, Internet of Things (IoT), Mobile Computing.

### 4. BS Data Science (BSDS)
* **Objective**: Teaches mathematical foundations, machine learning algorithms, statistical inference, and big data architectures to extract actionable insights from structured and unstructured data.
* **Core Curriculum**:
  * DS Domain Core: Linear Algebra, Multivariate Calculus, Probability & Statistics for Data Science, Data Mining, Machine Learning, Big Data Analytics, Deep Learning, Data Visualization.
  * Electives: Natural Language Processing, Business Process Intelligence, Financial Analytics, Reinforcement Learning.

### 5. BS Artificial Intelligence (BSAI)
* **Objective**: Focuses on modern cognitive systems, autonomous decision-making agents, generative models, and computer vision.

## Postgraduate Programs

### MS / MPhil Programs (2-Year / 30-36 Credit Hours)
* **Degrees**: MS Computer Science (MSCS), MS Software Engineering (MSSE), MS Data Science (MSDS).
* **Structure**: Coursework (24 Credit Hours) followed by a 6-to-12 Credit Hour Research Thesis defended before an external evaluation board.
* **Research Domains**: Computer Vision & Medical Imaging, Machine Learning, Distributed Cloud Systems, Natural Language Processing, and Process Analytics.

### PhD Computer Science (3 to 5 Years)
* **Requirements**: Comprehensive Examination, HEC-stipulated coursework, publication of peer-reviewed journal articles in HEC-recognized W/X category journals, and international dissertation defense.

## Academic Grading Scale & Regulations
* PUCIT operates on a 4.00 Grade Point Average (GPA) system.
* Continuous evaluation framework:
  * Assignments, Quizzes & Lab Tasks: 20% – 25%
  * Mid-Term Examination: 35%
  * Final Semester Examination: 40% – 45%
* Minimum CGPA to graduate: 2.00 for BS programs; 2.50 for MS programs; 3.00 for PhD.
