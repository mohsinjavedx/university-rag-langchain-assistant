# Administration and Campus Management — PUCIT / FCIT New Campus

The administrative and operational backbone of PUCIT / FCIT Quaid-i-Azam Campus (New Campus) ensures the smooth delivery of academic programs, physical infrastructure maintenance, safety protocols, and student support services across its expansive facilities.

---

## Central Administrative Leadership

### Central Administrator / Principal Office
* **Office Location**: Executive Administrative Wing, FCIT New Campus Building, Samsani Road, Quaid-i-Azam Campus, Lahore.
* **Supervision**: Oversees general administrative affairs, inter-departmental scheduling, staff human resources, campus budget execution, and security logistics.
* **Staff Members**:
  * **Assistant Registrar / Administrative Officer**: Handles daily campus operational filings, staff attendance, official notifications, and university statutory correspondence.
  * **Superintendent (Admin)**: Manages official dispatch, record archives, leave approvals, and non-teaching staff rosters.
  * **Personal Assistant (PA) to Administrator / Dean**: Manages executive appointments, meeting schedules, official visitor protocol, and deanery correspondence.

### Dean's Secretariat (FCIT)
* **Office Location**: Dean Office, Quaid-i-Azam Campus, Lahore.
* **Key Responsibilities**:
  * Coordination across the four constituent departments (CS, SE, IT, Data Science).
  * Implementation of Academic Council and Syndicate directives.
  * Faculty recruitment processing, visiting teacher contracts, and performance appraisals.
  * Liaison with the Vice Chancellor’s Secretariat, Registrar Office, and Treasurer Office of the University of the Punjab.

---

## Campus Management & Operational Committees

### New Campus Management Committee (NCMC)
* **Mandate**: Responsible for spatial allocation, lecture hall scheduling, maintenance of green campus grounds (including the campus fruit orchards and parkland), and campus civic upkeep.
* **Key Operational Staff**:
  * **Estate & Maintenance Supervisor**: Conducts daily inspections of classroom air conditioning, electrical backups (high-capacity diesel generators), water filtration plants, and building civil works.
  * **Sanitation & Custodial Staff Supervisor**: Coordinates hygiene, waste disposal, and routine disinfection across lecture halls, laboratory floors, and faculty lounges.
  * **Security Incharge & Security Guards**: Manages campus gated access (Samsani Road entrance), visitor ID verification, surveillance cameras (CCTV control room), and 24/7 campus perimeter protection.

### Day Care Center & Facilities Staff
* **Day Care Center Staff**: Operates the dedicated New Campus Day Care facility established to support faculty and staff working mothers.
* **Cafeteria & Canteen Staff**: Oversees hygiene standards, meal pricing, and canteen operations situated adjacent to the academic blocks.
* **Transport Coordinators**: Liaises with the central University of the Punjab Transport Wing to manage the university bus fleet ("Points") servicing Quaid-i-Azam Campus from all sectors of Lahore.
