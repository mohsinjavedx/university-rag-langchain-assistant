# Library and Learning Resource Staff — PUCIT / FCIT New Campus

The PUCIT / FCIT New Campus Library serves as a central hub of academic learning, research reference, and quiet study for undergraduate students, postgraduates, and faculty members. In addition to the specialized departmental library, students also enjoy full access to the iconic Punjab University Central Library at Quaid-i-Azam Campus.

---

## Library Staff & Key Responsibilities

### Incharge Library / Chief Librarian
* **Role**: Administers library acquisitions, cataloging of computing literature, digital database subscriptions, budget allocation, and inter-library lending arrangements.
* **Location**: FCIT Departmental Library, Quaid-i-Azam Campus.

### Assistant Librarian / Cataloger
* **Key Tasks**:
  * Manages the automated library information system (MLIMs / Koha integrated library system) for book check-ins, check-outs, and barcode labeling.
  * Classifies new computing text acquisitions according to Dewey Decimal Classification (DDC).
  * Coordinates inventory audits, book condition checks, and replacement requisitions for high-demand course textbooks.

### Digital Resources & HEC Digital Library Coordinator
* **Role**:
  * Guides students and researchers in accessing the HEC National Digital Library portal.
  * Facilitates access to premier research repositories: IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, ScienceDirect, SpringerLink, and Web of Science.
  * Conducts periodic Information Literacy and citation formatting workshops (utilizing tools such as EndNote, Zotero, and Mendeley).

### Circulation Desk & Reading Hall Attendants
* **Tasks**:
  * Issues student library cards and registers book borrowing privileges.
  * Enforces reading room discipline, quiet zones, and laptop desk etiquette.
  * Manages the physical reserve shelf containing single-copy reference textbooks and past Final Year Project (FYDP) documentation copies.

## Library Facilities & Services

| Service | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Physical Holdings** | Over 15,000+ print volumes in Computer Science, Software Engineering, AI, Mathematics, Networks, and Electronics. |
| **Digital Terminals** | Dedicated computers with high-speed internet access for browsing e-journals and downloading IEEE/ACM papers. |
| **Study Spaces** | Air-conditioned reading halls, individual study carrels, and collaborative discussion cubicles. |
| **Circulation Rules** | Undergraduate students may borrow up to 3 books simultaneously for a duration of 14 days, renewable once if not reserved. |
| **Borrowing Hours** | Monday – Friday: 08:30 AM to 04:00 PM. |

## Synergy with Punjab University Central Library
Students at the New Campus are also members of the central Punjab University Library (Quaid-e-Azam Campus), which spans over 102,000 square feet with seating for 2,500+ readers and collections exceeding 500,000 books, making it one of the largest academic libraries in South Asia.
