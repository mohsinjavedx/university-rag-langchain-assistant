# Student Affairs, Financial Aid and Project Office Staff — PUCIT / FCIT New Campus

Student-facing administrative operations at PUCIT / FCIT Quaid-i-Azam Campus are coordinated through specialized administrative wings: the Student Affairs & Admissions Office, the Financial Aid Committee, and the Final Year Design Project (FYDP) Project Office.

---

## 1. Final Year Design Project (FYDP) Office Staff

The FYDP Office is a centralized administrative and academic cell that oversees the multi-stage completion of capstone software projects for all graduating BS students across Computer Science, Software Engineering, IT, and Data Science.

* **Office Lead**: Dr. Muddassira Arshad (Head of FYDP Office).
* **Project Office Coordinator & Administrative Assistant**:
  * Receives, catalogs, and processes capstone project proposals submitted by student teams.
  * Ensures compliance with standardized IEEE/PUCIT templates for Software Requirements Specifications (SRS), Software Design Specifications (SDS), and Final Project Reports.
  * Schedules mid-term prototype demonstrations and final defense panels involving internal supervisors and external industry evaluators.
  * Coordinates the annual Capstone Project Exhibition / SoftExpo, connecting graduating batches with software house recruiters and industry judges.

---

## 2. Student Affairs & Admissions Office Staff

* **Admissions Incharge & Staff Officers**:
  * Coordinate the annual undergraduate and postgraduate admissions intake for both Morning and Self-Supporting (Afternoon/Replica) shifts.
  * Formulate and verify computerized merit lists based on academic metrics (Matriculation/O-Level, FSc Pre-Engineering / ICS / A-Level scores, and PU Entry Test results).
  * Manage document scrutiny desks, verifying original educational certificates, CNIC/B-Forms, and domicile affidavits.
  * Issue student registration numbers and official student ID cards.
* **Information & Public Inquiry Desk**: Provides advisory counseling on eligibility criteria, fee structures, shift selection, and quota seats (Sports, Hafiz-e-Quran, Disabled, Provincial, and Overseas quotas).

---

## 3. Financial Aid & Scholarship Branch Staff

PUCIT / FCIT maintains a steadfast institutional commitment ensuring that no deserving student is deprived of higher education due to financial distress.

* **Financial Aid Committee Secretary & Staff**:
  * Dispatches notices and processes applications for institutional fee concessions and kinship waivers.
  * Facilitates national and international scholarship programs:
    * **HEC Need-Based Scholarships**: Full tuition fee coverage plus monthly living stipends.
    * **Punjab Educational Endowment Fund (PEEF) Scholarships**: Awarded to high-achieving underprivileged students from across the province.
    * **PUCIT Alumni Financial Aid**: Direct emergency grants and tuition relief sponsored by overseas alumni and alumni endowments.
    * **Mora / Zakat Scholarships**: Processed in liaison with District Zakat Committees.
  * Coordinates physical home verification checks, interview schedules with the Scholarship Scrutiny Board, and timely account credit reconciliations.
