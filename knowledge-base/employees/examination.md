# Examination and Academic Branch — PUCIT / FCIT New Campus

The Examination and Academic Branch at PUCIT / FCIT New Campus manages student course registrations, semester mid-term and final examinations, re-sit schedules, grade calculation, academic transcript issuance, and degree clearance processes.

---

## Organizational Structure & Key Personnel

### Controller / Coordinator of Examinations
* **Role**: Supervises overall examination integrity, exam hall allocations, external examiner appointments, and adherence to semester examination regulations of the University of the Punjab.
* **Location**: Examination Office, Ground Floor, Academic Block, New Campus.

### Assistant Controller of Examinations / Superintendent
* **Key Responsibilities**:
  * Scheduling semester midterm and final exam date sheets for Morning and Self-Supporting shifts.
  * Formulating and releasing notifications for semester re-sit examinations.
  * Coordinating with course instructors to ensure timely grade book submissions.
  * Maintaining computerized academic records and CGPA calculations on the university portal.

### Academic Record & Transcript Clerks
* **Key Tasks**:
  * Processing student requests for official Semester Grade Reports (SGRs) and provisional transcripts.
  * Handling character certificates, bonafide student certificates, and English proficiency letters.
  * Managing verification dossiers sent from international universities, WES (World Education Services), and employer background checking agencies.

### Unfair Means & Cheating Committee (UMCC) Secretariat
* **Secretariat Staff**: Records incident reports from exam invigilators, prepares hearing files, schedules disciplinary committee sessions, and dispatches verdict notifications in compliance with university statutory rules.

---

## Core Operational Procedures

### 1. Semester Examination Cycles
* **Mid-Term Exams**: Conducted during the 8th or 9th week of the semester.
* **Final Exams**: Conducted during the 16th to 18th week of the semester.
* **Re-Sit Examinations**: Announced at the beginning of each semester for students looking to clear failed courses or improve grades in eligible coursework.

### 2. Transcript & Degree Clearance Workflow
1. **Departmental Clearance**: Obtain signed clearance forms from FYDP Project Office, IT Network Labs, Departmental Library, and Seminar Library.
2. **Accounts Clearance**: Fee voucher reconciliation confirming zero outstanding dues across all active semesters.
3. **Hostel & Sports Clearance**: Signed verification from New Campus Hostel Warden and Directorate of Sports.
4. **Final Degree Application**: Submission to the central PU Examination Department located at the New Campus Admin Block (P.O. Box 54590, Lahore).
