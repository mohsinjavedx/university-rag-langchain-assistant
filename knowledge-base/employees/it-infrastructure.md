# IT Infrastructure, Network Support and Laboratory Staff — PUCIT / FCIT New Campus

The technical operations at PUCIT / FCIT Quaid-i-Azam Campus are managed by a dedicated team of network engineers, systems administrators, lab instructors, and hardware technicians. They maintain the college's server rooms, high-performance computing clusters, fiber connectivity, and computer laboratories.

---

## Technical Teams & Roles

### 1. Network Operations & Data Center Staff
* **Network Administrator / Systems Engineer**:
  * Oversees the campus fiber backbone connecting the New Campus academic blocks, lecture theatres, and GIS Centre.
  * Manages core switches, routers, VLAN partitioning, campus-wide Wi-Fi access points, and firewall rules.
  * Interfaces with the central Punjab University Information Technology Centre (IT-Centre) for high-speed PERN (Pakistan Education and Research Network) internet bandwidth.
* **Server & Cloud Infrastructure Engineers**:
  * Administers internal virtualization servers (VMware / Proxmox clusters), web servers hosting departmental portals, and local code repositories.
  * Configures DNS, DHCP, Active Directory, student university email domains (`@pucit.edu.pk`), and LDAP authentication for lab terminals.

### 2. Computer Laboratory Administrators & Staff
Each computer lab at the New Campus is equipped with 40 to 60 high-performance workstations running dual-boot operating systems (Ubuntu Linux and Windows 10/11 Pro).
* **Lab Incharges / Technical Officers**:
  * Maintain operational readiness of desktop machines, monitors, UPS systems, and networking switches in designated labs.
  * Deploy academic software stacks: compilers (GCC, Clang), IDEs (Visual Studio Code, IntelliJ IDEA, PyCharm), database engines (PostgreSQL, MySQL, Oracle), and data science toolkits (Anaconda, Jupyter, TensorFlow, PyTorch).
  * Enforce lab conduct, workstation logging, and equipment safety protocols.
* **Hardware & Maintenance Technicians**:
  * Perform preventive maintenance, diagnostic checks, RAM upgrades, SSD replacements, and projector installations across all lecture theatres.

### 3. Specialized Labs Supported
* **GIS Laboratory**: Technical support for Geographic Information System workstations running ArcGIS and remote sensing software suites.
* **Artificial Intelligence & Data Science Lab**: Equipped with high-end GPU workstations for deep learning model training and big data processing.
* **Software Engineering Design Studio**: Tailored for collaborative team sprints, agile project demos, and UI/UX design tools.
* **Microprocessor & Embedded Systems Lab**: Houses FPGA kits, microcontroller boards, digital oscilloscopes, and logic analyzers.
