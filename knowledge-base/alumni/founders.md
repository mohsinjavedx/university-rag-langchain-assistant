# Notable Tech Founders and Industry Leaders — PUCIT / FCIT Alumni

PUCIT alumni have founded and led dozens of software enterprises, venture-backed technology startups, digital service agencies, and research consultancies across Pakistan, the UAE, the UK, and Silicon Valley.

---

## Startup Founders & Tech Innovators

### 1. Artificial Intelligence & Enterprise Solutions
* **Dr. Adnan Abid & Alumni Founders — Techtics AI Pvt Ltd**:
  * Founded to build enterprise-scale Artificial Intelligence solutions, intelligent knowledge extraction engines, and medical diagnostics platforms ([Techtics AI Profile](https://sst.umt.edu.pk/news/International-Conference-on-AI-and-Emerging-Technologies-ICAIET-2025.aspx)).
  * Employs dozens of PUCIT graduates in AI engineering, MLOps, and full-stack development roles.
* **Hamza Kamran — Zaaric**:
  * Founded by PUCIT graduate and Qimam Fellow Hamza Kamran ([Qimam Alumni](https://qimam.com/pk/alumni/)).
  * Delivers agentic AI architectures and full-stack software systems addressing automation challenges in healthcare, retail, and fintech across the MENA and Pakistani markets.

### 2. Software Outsourcing & Product Development Houses
PUCIT graduates are prominent across Lahore’s technology hubs, including Arfa Software Technology Park, Johar Town, and Gulberg tech corridors:
* **Custom Software Consultancies**: Multiple digital transformation agencies and web/mobile engineering studios founded by PUCIT alumni provide high-value services to European and North American clients, specializing in Node.js, Python/Django, React/React Native, and cloud microservices.
* **Fintech & Payment Gateway Specialists**: Alumni have developed proprietary middleware for automated invoice reconciliation, micro-lending algorithms, and mobile wallet integrations in collaboration with local banking partners.
* **Gaming & Interactive Media Studios**: Independent game development studios founded by PUCIT alumni specializing in Unity 3D and Unreal Engine hyper-casual and simulation games that have amassed millions of downloads on Google Play and Apple App Store.

## Incubation & Startup Acceleration Ties

### Plan9 & PITB Incubation Synergy
* In the formative days of Pakistan’s tech startup boom under the Punjab Information Technology Board (PITB) and the establishment of **Plan9** (Pakistan’s largest technology incubator) and **PlanX**, numerous founding teams were spearheaded by PUCIT graduates.
* The proximity of PUCIT to Arfa Software Technology Park has fostered a continuous flow of graduating students transforming final-year projects (FYDPs) into viable commercial products.

### PUCIT Software Technology Park (STP)
* The on-campus Software Technology Park at Quaid-i-Azam Campus was specifically designed to provide subsidized office spaces, gigabit fiber connections, and electricity backups to alumni-led startups, retaining top student talent within the university ecosystem ([PUCIT Software Technology Park](https://pu.edu.pk/home/section/press/8766)).
