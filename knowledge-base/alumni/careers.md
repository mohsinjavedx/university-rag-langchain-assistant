# Global Tech Workforce and Corporate Placement — PUCIT / FCIT Alumni

Graduates of PUCIT / FCIT represent a core pillar of the technology workforce in Pakistan and are widely employed across multinational technology corporations, financial institutions, and global cloud providers.

---

## Employment in Major Pakistani Technology Companies

PUCIT alumni represent one of the largest talent pools in the domestic software export industry:
* **Systems Limited**: Hundreds of alumni serve as Principal Consultants, Solution Architects, Data Engineers, and Vice Presidents across Systems Limited’s enterprise software, cloud, and BPO verticals.
* **NetSol Technologies**: Alumni lead development and maintenance of lease and finance software platforms serving Fortune 500 automotive clients globally.
* **Arbisoft**: Prominent presence of PUCIT engineers building scalable educational technology (edX core infrastructure), travel tech, and scraping pipelines.
* **Confiz & Retailistan**: Leading engineering initiatives in supply chain automation, retail SaaS platforms, and enterprise Azure/AWS cloud migrations.
* **10Pearls & VentureDive**: Engineering digital experiences, mobile applications, and AI integrations for international clients.
* **Afiniti & TRG Pakistan**: Alumni work as artificial intelligence engineers and speech analytics specialists optimizing call center routing algorithms.

---

## Global Big Tech & International Footprint

PUCIT graduates hold senior engineering and leadership positions across tier-1 international tech firms:
* **Google**: Software Engineers, Site Reliability Engineers (SREs), and Engineering Managers across Google offices in Zurich, Dublin, London, Mountain View, and Singapore.
* **Microsoft**: Senior Program Managers, Cloud Solution Architects, and Developers working on Microsoft 365, Azure Cloud Infrastructure, and Developer Division tools in Redmond and European campuses.
* **Amazon & AWS**: Distributed systems engineers, backend developers, and solutions architects handling AWS cloud services, Prime logistics software, and DynamoDB storage engines in Dublin, Berlin, Luxembourg, and Seattle.
* **Meta (Facebook)**: Core software engineers optimizing distributed social graph queries, video streaming pipelines, and advertisement ranking models in London and Menlo Park.
* **Apple & Oracle**: Database kernel developers, iOS framework engineers, and enterprise ERP consultants in the US and Europe.

## Corporate Recruitment Channels at PUCIT
* **Annual FCIT Career Fair**: Over 60 to 80 software companies visit the Quaid-i-Azam Campus annually to conduct written technical assessments and on-spot interviews for graduating students.
* **Alumni Referral Pipeline**: The extensive PUCIT alumni Slack, WhatsApp, and LinkedIn networks actively post international and local job referrals, giving graduates an immediate head start in competitive hiring pipelines.
