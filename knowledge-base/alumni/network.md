# PUCIT Alumni Network and Associations — PUCIT / FCIT

With thousands of computer science, software engineering, and information technology graduates since its inception in 2001, PUCIT / FCIT boasts one of the most cohesive, influential, and supportive alumni ecosystems in Pakistan's technology landscape.

---

## Organizational Structure & Alumni Platforms

### 1. PUCIT Alumni Initiative
* **Official Portal**: [PUCIT Alumni Initiative](https://www.pucitalumni.com/)
* **Mission**: Reconnect graduates across global chapters (Pakistan, North America, Europe, Middle East, and Australia), drive collaborative industrial ventures, and offer structured mentorship to enrolled undergraduates.
* **Core Activities**:
  * **Global Mentorship Program**: Pairing 3rd and 4th-year students with seasoned software architects, product managers, and engineering managers abroad.
  * **Alumni Emergency Endowment**: Raising financial assistance funds to cover tuition for financially distressed PUCIT students.
  * **Mock Technical Interviews**: Organizing behavioral and data structures/algorithms mock interviews for graduating seniors ahead of the FCIT Career Fair.

### 2. Punjab University Central Alumni Office
* **Office Location**: Alumni Office, Quaid-i-Azam Campus (New Campus), Lahore ([PU Alumni Office](https://alumni.pu.edu.pk/)).
* **Email**: `info.alumni@pu.edu.pk`

* **Student Society**: PU Alumni Student Society coordinates grand alumni dinners at Faisal Auditorium and campus reunions.
* **Alumni Privileges**: Access to university research libraries, invitations to academic symposiums, guest speaker series, and discounted campus sports facility passes.

---

## Annual Reunions & Industry Reconnection
* **Annual Alumni Homecoming & Dinner**: Held annually during the winter season (November/December), bringing together founding batches and recent graduates to celebrate accomplishments, network, and exchange industry trends.
* **Alumni Talks & Masterclasses**: Technical workshops hosted at the New Campus lecture halls where alumni flying in from abroad deliver masterclasses on LLM fine-tuning, cloud-native deployments, Kubernetes at scale, and high-frequency trading systems.

---

## Financial Aid & Endowments by Alumni
The PUCIT alumni community has a long-standing tradition of giving back:
* **Direct Tuition Grants**: Every semester, the alumni endowment finances dozens of full tuition fee grants for deserving morning and self-supporting students.
* **Hardware & Lab Sponsorships**: Past batches have sponsored hardware upgrades, GPU compute nodes for AI research labs, and book endowments for the New Campus Library.
